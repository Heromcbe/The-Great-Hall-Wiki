# Server Settings

Wyrmwood is tuned for a small group that wants to enjoy ARK without the heavy grind. Here's what's different from official servers.

## Rates

| Setting | Wyrmwood |
|---------|----------|
| **Taming** | 5× faster |
| **Harvesting** | 5× more resources per hit |
| **Resource respawn** | About 2.5× faster |
| **Wild creatures** | 1.5× more of them |
| **Supply drops** | Custom drops |

## Breeding

| Setting | Wyrmwood |
|---------|----------|
| **Mating cooldown** | 20× shorter |
| **Egg hatching** | 10× faster |
| **Baby growth** | 10× faster |
| **Imprint cuddles** | Come 10× more often |
| **Imprint per cuddle** | 2× more |

## Survival

| Setting | Wyrmwood |
|---------|----------|
| **Hunger (players and tames)** | Drains slower |
| **Crops** | Grow faster |
| **Fuel** | Lasts longer |
| **Bodies and dropped items** | Stay around longer before they despawn |
| **Swim speed** | Leveling oxygen boosts swim speed more than usual |
| **Fortitude** | Each point does more for players |

## Tamed Creatures

Tames gain **more per level** in health, stamina, oxygen, food, and carry weight than on official servers.

## Quality of Life

- Pick up any structure you placed, at any time
- Building in caves is allowed (except artifact caves; see the [rules](index.md#rules))
- Unlimited stat respecs
- Flyers can carry creatures
- Cryopods work without a Cryofridge, and Cryofridges can go on platform saddles
- Your location shows on the map
- Third-person view, crosshair, hit markers, and damage numbers are on
- Up to 3 bunkers per tribe
- Idle players are kicked after about an hour
