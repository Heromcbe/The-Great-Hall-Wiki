# Wyrmwood FAQ

??? question "How do I join Wyrmwood?"
    Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/PNdRF6eg5j) and complete onboarding. Your onboarding ticket has the server details and password. Neither is ever posted publicly.

??? question "Do I need to install the mods?"
    No. Mods download automatically when you join the server.

??? question "Why can't I spend engram points?"
    You don't need to. [Auto Engrams!](mods/auto-engrams.md) unlocks engrams automatically as you level up.

??? question "I can't find an item in my crafting menu."
    Check your level. With Auto Engrams, items unlock when you reach the level they require. Some items are also crafted at a specific station rather than from your inventory.

??? question "Is Wyrmwood PvP?"
    No. It's a PvE server focused on playing together.

??? question "Where do I find a Phoenix or a Rock Drake?"
    Phoenixes and Reapers spawn in the Dunes. Rock Drakes live in the Wyvern Cave. See [Creatures](creatures.md).

??? question "How many creatures can I keep?"
    There's no set number, but large herds cause lag on a small server. If you have lots of creatures sitting around doing nothing, it's time to cull. Cryopods from [Super Cryo Storage](mods/super-cryo-storage.md) help.

??? question "Can I build in an artifact cave?"
    You can run them, but don't build inside them. Don't wall off resources, spawn points, caves, or obelisks either.

??? question "The server seems to be down."
    Check the Wyrmwood channel in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/PNdRF6eg5j) for announcements. After an ARK update, the server may need a little time to catch up.
