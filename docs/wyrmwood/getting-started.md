# Getting Started

New to Wyrmwood? Here's what's different from a normal ARK server.

## Before You Join

1. Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/PNdRF6eg5j) and complete onboarding. Your ticket has the server details and password.
2. Launch ARK: Survival Ascended and join using those details. The server's mods download automatically.

## What's Different Here

- **No engram points to spend.** [Auto Engrams!](mods/auto-engrams.md) unlocks everything automatically as you level up.
- **Resources stack to 10,000 and weigh 90% less,** thanks to [TG Stacking](mods/tg-stacking.md). Gathering trips go a lot further.
- **Better spyglass.** Craft a [Super Spyglass Plus](mods/super-spyglass-plus.md) early. It shows exactly what a creature eats and how good its stats are before you tame it.
- **Extra creatures on Ragnarok,** including Phoenix, Reapers, and Rock Drakes. See [Creatures](creatures.md).
- **Better building and automation** with [Cybers Structures](mods/cybers-structures.md) and [Arkitect Structures](mods/arkitect-structures.md).
- **Easy creature storage** with [Super Cryo Storage](mods/super-cryo-storage.md), from level 51.

## Playing Together

Wyrmwood is a small PvE server for up to 8 players, built around working together:

- Coordinate **boss fights** in the Discord so everyone gets a chance at progression.
- Don't loot other players' death bags unless they ask you to.
- Don't lead wild creatures into someone's base.

Read the [Wyrmwood rules](index.md#rules) before you start building.
