# Super Spyglass Plus

A much better spyglass. Look at any creature and see everything you need to know before you tame, breed, or fight it.

## What It Shows

- **Stats:** a creature's wild or tamed stats, including mutation counts
- **Taming:** exactly which food it wants and how much you need
- **Colors:** the creature's colors by region
- **Buffs:** active buffs and how long they have left
- **Breeding:** gene traits, fertilized egg info, and details on babies a mother is carrying
- **Location:** your coordinates and compass direction

It can also scan supply drops, dropped item bags, and structures.

## How to Get It

Unlock the Super Spyglass engram and craft it. With [Auto Engrams!](auto-engrams.md), it unlocks on its own as you level.

## How to Use It

You can use it three ways: hold it like a normal spyglass, wear it as a head armor skin, or toggle it on and off.

| Action (PC) | What it does |
|-------------|--------------|
| Melee button | Lock onto a target |
| Alt + mouse wheel | Toggle scan and zoom |
| Alt + Home | Open the spyglass settings |
| Shift + F3 / F4 / F5 | Predator vision, night vision, outline mode |

In the settings you can change the colors, text size, position, and crosshair style.
