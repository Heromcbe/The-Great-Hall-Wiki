# Mods

Wyrmwood runs a small set of quality-of-life mods. They make the game smoother without changing what ARK is about.

!!! tip "No setup needed"
    Mods download automatically when you join the server. You don't need to install anything yourself.

## Quality of Life

| Mod | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| [TG Stacking Mod 10000-90](tg-stacking.md) | Resources stack to 10,000 and weigh 90% less |
| [Super Spyglass Plus](super-spyglass-plus.md) | Shows a creature's stats, mutations, taming food, and more |
| [Auto Engrams!](auto-engrams.md) | Unlocks engrams automatically as you level up |

## Building & Automation

| Mod | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| [Cybers Structures QoL+](cybers-structures.md) | Better building pieces plus automated terminals for crafting, breeding, and farming |
| [Arkitect Structures Remastered](arkitect-structures.md) | Lots of extra building shapes and decorations |

## Creature Management & Spawns

| Mod | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| [Super Cryo Storage](super-cryo-storage.md) | Faster cryopods plus a terminal that collects babies, eggs, and resources |
| [Ragnarok Bonus Spawns](../creatures.md) | Adds Phoenix, Reapers, Rock Drakes, and more to Ragnarok |
