# Super Cryo Storage

Faster cryopods and a terminal that takes care of your breeding and farming.

## What It Adds

Three items, unlocked at **level 51**:

| Item | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **SCS Gun** | Captures creatures quickly |
| **SCS Collection** | Holds your stored creatures |
| **SCS Terminal** | Base terminal that automates breeding and resources |

## Storing & Releasing Creatures

- Use the **SCS Gun** to store a creature.
- Buffs stay on a creature when you store and release it.
- Release creatures one after another without opening your inventory.

## The SCS Terminal

Place it in your base and it will:

- Collect baby creatures
- Collect and hatch fertilized eggs
- Produce resources over time, such as unfertilized eggs, poop, and Achatina paste
- Spread fertilizer to nearby crop plots

**Tip:** egg production goes up when you have pairs of a species nearby, and even more with an Oviraptor around. Dung beetles in the terminal turn poop into fertilizer.
