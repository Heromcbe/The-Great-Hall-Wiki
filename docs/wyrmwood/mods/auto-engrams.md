# Auto Engrams!

No more spending engram points. Engrams unlock automatically as you level up.

## What It Does

- When you hit the level an engram needs, it **unlocks on its own**. Nothing to click.
- **Engram points aren't needed** for unlocks.
- It works for **mod engrams too**, including the building and cryo mods on this server.

## Good to Know

- If you're looking for an item and can't find it in your crafting menu, check your level first. It unlocks when you reach the level it requires.
- Engrams that the server has hidden or disabled stay locked.
