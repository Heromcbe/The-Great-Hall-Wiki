# Arkitect Structures Remastered

For builders who want more shapes and more style.

## What It Adds

- **New shapes:** diagonal foundations and ceilings, sloped pillars, curved roofs, more stair types, huge "Behemoth" walls, and 4x4 gates
- **Special foundations:** floating foundations for water and anti-grav foundations for building in mid-air
- **Built-in variants:** cycle through looks while placing a piece, no skins needed
- **Glass:** adjustable color, opacity, and lattice style

## Handy Tools

- **Structure Gun:** pick up, demolish, adjust, and repair structures from a distance
- **Paint Sprayer:** paint connected structures in one go, without using paint
- **Lights:** switch on and off with day and night, in any color
- **Televators and Propulsers:** move between floors, or launch yourself across your base

## How to Get Pieces

Pieces unlock with normal engram progression through every tier, from Thatch to Tek. With [Auto Engrams!](auto-engrams.md), they unlock as you level. The **Personal Crafter** lets you craft early-tier pieces anywhere.
