## Rules

The [Community Rules](../network/community-rules.md) apply here, plus the game rules below. This is a summary of **Section 6** of the [full rulebook (PDF)](https://github.com/Heromcbe/The-Great-Hall-Docs/blob/main/The_Great_Hall_Rules.pdf), which is the official version.

These are PvE community servers built around playing and building together.

**Teleporters & private spaces**

- Clearly label any teleporter that leads into your base.
- If a teleporter drops you somewhere private, leave right away and tell the owner.
- Don't change or rearrange someone else's base unless you're invited.

**Building & performance**

- Mega builds are welcome, as long as they don't lag the server.
- Builds shouldn't block paths or interfere with other players' gameplay.
