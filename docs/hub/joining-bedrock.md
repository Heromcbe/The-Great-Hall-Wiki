# Joining on Bedrock & Console

Bedrock players (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, mobile, and Windows) join the Hub through our friend bot, **TheGreatHall654**. Once it's on your friends list, the Hub shows up in your Friends tab like a friend's world.

!!! warning "Onboarding first"
    You can visit the Hub without being whitelisted, but you won't be able to travel to our servers until you've finished [Discord onboarding](../network/join-discord.md).

## Add the Friend Bot

1. Open Minecraft and sign in with your Microsoft account.
2. Go to **Play → Friends**.
3. Select **Add Friend** and search for the gamertag **TheGreatHall654**.
4. Send the friend request. The bot accepts automatically, which can take a minute.

You can also add **TheGreatHall654** from the Xbox app or an Xbox console. Any way works, as long as it's the same Microsoft account you play Minecraft with.

## Join the Hub

1. In Minecraft, go to **Play → Friends**.
2. Look for **TheGreatHall654** under your joinable friends.
3. Select it and join. You'll land on the Hub island.

## Mobile & Windows: Direct Connect

If you'd rather add the server directly, use the details in your Discord onboarding ticket under **Play → Servers → Add Server**.

## Good to Know

- **Your name has a dot in front of it** (for example, `.PlayerName`). That's normal for Bedrock players on our crossplay servers.
- To travel, walk up to an NPC and **interact** with it: right-click on PC, the left trigger on a controller, or tap on mobile.

Having trouble? See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
