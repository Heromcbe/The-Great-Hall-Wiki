# Getting Started

New to Aethelgard? Here's what to do on your first day.

## 1. Read the Rules

Aethelgard follows the [Community Rules](../network/community-rules.md) plus its own survival rules. The short version is on the [Aethelgard page](index.md#rules).

## 2. Look Around Spawn

Spawn is home to:

- **The market:** NPCs that buy your ores for EP
- **The Return to Hub NPCs:** for hopping to other servers

## 3. Find Your Spot and Claim It

The world is about 10,000 × 10,000 blocks. Scout a spot on the [Live Map](live-map.md), then claim it before you build:

```
/claim
```

Claiming costs EP per chunk. See [Land Claims](land-claims.md).

!!! tip "Claim before you build"
    Unclaimed builds are at your own risk.

## 4. Start Earning EP

- **Mine ores** and sell them at the market.
- **Sell items** to other players' shops.
- **Open your own shop** once you have goods to sell. See [Running a Shop](shops/running-a-shop.md).

Selling pays you in **Deposit Coins**. Run `/deposit` to add them to your balance, and `/bal` to check it. See [Electrum (EP)](economy/index.md).

## 5. Know What Happens When You Die

Your items go into a private **grave chest** where you died. Use `/grave where` to find it. See [Graves](graves.md).

## 6. Say Hi in Discord

In-game chat is linked to Discord, so you can keep up with everyone even when you're offline. See [Discord Link](discord-link.md).
