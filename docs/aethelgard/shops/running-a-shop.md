# Running a Shop

Set up your own shopkeeper to sell your goods, buy items from other players, or both.

## 1. Get a Shop-Creation Egg

You need a special **Villager Spawn Egg** named **Shopkeeper** (in green). It can only create a shop; it won't spawn a real villager.

!!! info "How to get one"
    Shop-creation eggs are handed out by staff for now. Ask a staff member for one. We're planning to sell them through a Bank vendor for EP.

## 2. Create Your Shop

1. **Place a new chest** for your shop's stock. It has to be a chest **you placed recently**; an old chest won't work.
2. Hold the egg and **right-click the air** (not a block) to cycle through the shop types. The chat tells you which one is selected.
3. Select **Trading Shop**.
4. **Right-click your chest**, then **right-click the block** where you want your shopkeeper to stand. It must be within **15 blocks** of the chest.
5. Your shopkeeper appears.

!!! warning "Keep your egg safe"
    Deleting a shop **does not give the egg back**. Place your shop carefully.

!!! warning "Use Trading Shop"
    Always choose **Trading Shop**. The other types (Buying, Selling, and Book Shop) still work, but they use **Emeralds** as payment and can't be set up to use EP or Deposit Coins.

### Change Its Look

Your shopkeeper looks like a Villager by default. **Sneak + right-click while holding the egg** to cycle through 87 other looks. It loops back to Villager after the last one. Shops can also be a **sign**, **hanging sign**, or **end crystal** (end crystals can't be placed in the End).

## 3. Set Up Your Trades

1. Open your chest and put the items you're trading inside.
2. **Shift + right-click** your shopkeeper to open the trade editor.
3. Your chest's items appear in the top row. Drag them down into the trade slots.

A Trading Shop can trade **any item for any item**. Diamonds for Emeralds with no EP involved works too. Most shops use coins so they can deal in Electrum:

### Selling to Players (you have goods, they pay you)

- Put the item you're selling in one slot.
- Put a **Deposit Coin** worth your price in the cost slot below it.

When a customer buys, their coin lands in your chest.

### Buying From Players (you want their goods, you pay them)

- Put the item you want to buy in one slot.
- Put one **Deposit Coin** worth what you'll pay in the result slot.
- Put a **Balance Coin** in your chest to fund it: `/coin balancecoin <amount>`.

Your shop keeps **2 of these coins stocked** at all times, paying for more out of your Balance Coin. When the Balance Coin runs dry, the trade shows as out of stock until you add funds.

## Shop Editor Options

Shift + right-click your shopkeeper to open the editor. Besides your trades (up to **5 pages**), it has these buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Name Tag | Rename your shop (3–25 letters, numbers, and spaces) |
| Chest | Open your shop's chest |
| Ender Pearl | Move your shopkeeper |
| Player Head | Add **members**: up to 9 players who can help run the shop |
| Bell | Turn trade notifications on or off. You're told when someone trades at your shop |
| Bone | Delete the shop |

## 4. Getting Paid

Coins from customers pile up as items in your chest. That's normal. They're turned into your **Balance Coin** automatically:

- as soon as **anyone closes that shop's menu** (including a customer finishing a purchase), or
- right away if your chest **fills up**, so a full chest never blocks the next sale.

Your shop usually sorts itself out right after each sale, without you visiting it.

### Cashing Out

As the owner, you can open your chest and take your own Balance Coin at any time. Then run `/deposit` to turn it into EP on your balance.

## Good to Know

- **No limit** on how many shops you can own.
- **No shop tax.** You keep everything your shop earns.
- **Your chest is protected.** Other players can't open or break it, and hoppers can't pull items out.
- **You can't trade with your own shop** (or a shop you're a member of).
- Breaking your chest doesn't delete the shop, but the shop has nothing to trade until you fix it.

## Tips

- **Give your chest room.** Each coin value takes its own slot, so a shop with 5 different prices can have 5+ coin stacks. Use a double chest if you run several trades.
- **Out of stock?** Either your Balance Coin is out of funds (for trades that buy from players), or your chest is full (for trades that sell).
