# Buying & Selling

Shops can trade in both directions: they can **sell** goods to you, and **buy** items from you.

## Buying From a Shop

1. Make sure you have enough **Deposit Coins**. If you need cash, run `/withdraw <amount>` to turn EP into a Deposit Coin.
2. Right-click the shopkeeper to open its trades.
3. Pick the trade you want. Your coin is taken and you get the item.

!!! tip "Carry the right coins"
    Each trade asks for a coin of a specific value. `/withdraw` the exact amount the trade asks for.

## Selling to a Shop

1. Right-click a shopkeeper that buys the item you have.
2. Pick the trade. Your item goes to the shop and you receive a **Deposit Coin**.
3. Run `/deposit` to cash in all your Deposit Coins at once.

The **market NPCs** buy ores the same way.

## "Out of Stock"?

A shop that shows a trade as out of stock is almost always either:

- a shop that **buys** items and has run out of money to pay with, or
- a shop that **sells** items and has a full chest that needs the owner to check in.

Try again later, or let the owner know.
