# Shops

Aethelgard's shops are run by **shopkeepers**: NPCs that trade on a player's behalf, backed by a chest of stock. Anyone can buy from a shop, and you can set up your own.

## How Money Works in Shops

Shops trade with **coins**, not your EP balance directly:

- **Deposit Coins** are spendable cash. You get them when you sell, and pay with them when you buy.
- **Balance Coins** fund a shop so it can buy items from players.

See [Aethelgard Coins](../economy/coins.md) for the full explanation.
