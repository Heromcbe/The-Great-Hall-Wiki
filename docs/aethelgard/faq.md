# Aethelgard FAQ

??? question "Can I play on Bedrock or console?"
    Yes. Aethelgard is Java + Bedrock crossplay. Join through The Great Hall Hub. See [Bedrock Players](bedrock-players.md).

??? question "Why does my name (or my friend's) start with a dot?"
    Bedrock players' names start with a dot on Aethelgard. Include the dot when you type their name in a command.

??? question "How do I earn EP?"
    Sell ores at the market at spawn, sell to other players' shops, or run your own shop. See [Electrum (EP)](economy/index.md).

??? question "How do I pay another player?"
    Run `/withdraw <amount>` to make a Deposit Coin, give it to them, and they run `/deposit`. There's no `/pay` command.

??? question "I sold something but my balance didn't go up."
    Shops pay in **Deposit Coins**. Run `/deposit` to cash them in. See [Aethelgard Coins](economy/coins.md).

??? question "Someone took my Balance Coin!"
    They can't keep it. Balance Coins are locked to you. If anyone else picks one up, its value goes straight back to your balance.

??? question "A shop says it's out of stock."
    The owner either needs to add money to the shop (for trades that buy from you) or empty a full chest (for trades that sell to you). Try again later or let the owner know.

??? question "How do I add a friend to my claim?"
    `/aclaim add <claim-name> <player>`, or `/aclaim add * <player>` for all your claims. See [Land Claims](land-claims.md).

??? question "I died. Where are my items?"
    In your grave chest where you died. Use `/grave where` or `/grave tp`. Only you (and staff) can open it. See [Graves](graves.md).

??? question "Can I buy VIP?"
    No. VIP is earned through gameplay, never bought. See [Ranks & VIP](ranks-and-vip.md).

??? question "Something's broken or I found a bug. Who do I tell?"
    Report it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/PNdRF6eg5j).
