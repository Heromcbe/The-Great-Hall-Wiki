# Ranks & VIP

Aethelgard is **not pay-to-win**. VIP is **earned through gameplay**, never bought.

!!! info "More details coming"
    How to earn VIP will be explained here closer to launch.

## What VIP Gets You

| Perk | Default | VIP |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost per claimed chunk** | 100 EP | 75 EP |
| **Max chunks in one claim** | 256 | 1,024 |
| **Max chunks in total** | 312 | 1,113 |
| **Members per claim** | 6 | 8 |
| **`/claim tp`** | 5-second wait | Instant |

See [Land Claims](land-claims.md) for how claiming works.
