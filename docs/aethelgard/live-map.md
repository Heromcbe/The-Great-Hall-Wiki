# Live Map

Aethelgard has a **live 3D map** you can explore from your browser.

[Open the Aethelgard map](http://map.thegreathall.world:8088/){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## What You Can Do

- **Fly around the world in 3D**, or switch to a flat top-down view.
- **Scout for a place to build** before you set out.
- **See land claims:** claims show on the map with their name and owner, so you can spot open land and keep a buffer from your neighbours.
- Browse the Overworld, the Nether, and the End.

!!! tip "Plan before you claim"
    Check the map for nearby claims before you pick a spot. See [Land Claims](land-claims.md).
