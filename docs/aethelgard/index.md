## Rules

The [Community Rules](../network/community-rules.md) apply here, plus the game rules below. This is a summary of **Section 4** of the [full rulebook (PDF)](https://github.com/Heromcbe/The-Great-Hall-Docs/blob/main/The_Great_Hall_Rules.pdf), which is the official version.

**Building & land**

- Claim your land before you build. Unclaimed builds are at your own risk.
- Leave a buffer next to other players' claims.
- Clean up after yourself: replant trees, fill in surface mining, and remove pillars and temporary scaffolding.
- No offensive builds.

**Griefing & theft**

- No griefing of any kind. Large-scale griefing is a permanent ban, and we have full logs and rollback.
- Don't touch containers you didn't place, even if you can open them.
- Don't kill other players' animals or pets.

**Redstone & farms**

- Keep redstone lag-friendly. If staff asks you to change a machine, do it.
- No zero-tick farms or lag machines. AFK farms must not hurt server performance.

**Economy & trading**

- Honor your trades. Keep your shops stocked and fairly priced.
- No market manipulation, no currency or item duplication, no stealing from shops.

**The Nether & The End**

- The Nether roof, End islands, and End cities are shared. Don't claim or block off big sections.
- Don't destroy portals that aren't yours, and don't hog End city loot.
