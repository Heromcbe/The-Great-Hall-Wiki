# Commands

Every player command on Aethelgard, in one place. On Bedrock, type these in chat the same way.

## Money

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/bal` | Check your EP balance |
| `/withdraw <amount>` | Turn EP into a Deposit Coin |
| `/deposit` | Cash in every Deposit Coin (and your own Balance Coins) in your inventory |
| `/coin balancecoin <amount>` | Make a Balance Coin to fund your shop |

See [Electrum (EP)](economy/index.md) and [Aethelgard Coins](economy/coins.md).

## Land Claims

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/claim` | Claim your starting area (4 × 4 chunks) |
| `/claim radius <n>` | Claim a square around you |
| `/claim addchunk` | Add the chunk you're standing in to your claim |
| `/claim merge` | Merge claims that touch |
| `/unclaim` | Unclaim the land you're standing on |
| `/claims` | Open a menu to manage your claims |
| `/claim see` | Show your claim's borders |
| `/claim seenear` | Show nearby claims' borders |
| `/claim tp` | Teleport to one of your claims |
| `/claim permissions` | Choose what members and visitors can do |
| `/claim flags` | Choose claim-wide settings |
| `/claim sell` | Sell a claim to another player |
| `/aclaim add <claim-name> <player>` | Add a member (`*` = all your claims) |
| `/aclaim remove <claim-name> <player>` | Remove a member (`*` = all your claims) |
| `/cc` | Claim chat |

See [Land Claims](land-claims.md).

## Graves

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/grave where` | Show where your most recent grave is |
| `/grave tp` | Teleport to your most recent grave |

See [Graves](graves.md).
