# Graves

When you die on Aethelgard, your items don't scatter on the ground. They're stored in a **grave chest** at the spot where you died.

## How It Works

- A **chest** appears where you died (or as close as it can safely go) with your items inside.
- Your grave is **private**. Only you and staff can open it. In **PvP areas**, the player who killed you can open it too.
- A floating label above it shows whose grave it is, what killed you, and how long the grave has left.
- The death message tells you **where you died**.
- Graves can't be blown up, pushed by pistons, or emptied by hoppers.
- The grave disappears once it's empty.

## Getting Your Items Back

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/grave where` | Shows where your most recent grave is |
| `/grave tp` | Teleports you to your most recent grave (short delay; moving cancels it) |

**Shift + right-click** your grave to put everything back into your inventory and equip your armor. Anything that doesn't fit stays in the grave.

!!! warning "Graves don't last forever"
    Graves expire after a while. Get back to yours as soon as you can.
