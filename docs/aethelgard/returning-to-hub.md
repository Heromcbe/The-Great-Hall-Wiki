# Returning to the Hub

Want to hop over to another Minecraft server? Head back to **The Great Hall Hub**.

## How to Get Back

Find the **Return to Hub** NPCs at **spawn**. There are two:

| NPC | Use it if you play on |
|---|---|
| **Return to Hub (Java)** | Java Edition |
| **Return to Hub (Bedrock)** | Bedrock: console, mobile, or Windows |

Interact with the one for your edition and you're sent back to the Hub. From there you can pick any Minecraft server. See [Server Select NPCs](../hub/server-select.md).

!!! tip "Or just rejoin"
    You can also leave Aethelgard and rejoin the Hub the normal way. See [Joining on Bedrock & Console](../hub/joining-bedrock.md) or [Joining on Java](../hub/joining-java.md).
