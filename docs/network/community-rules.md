[Read the full rulebook](https://github.com/Heromcbe/The-Great-Hall-Docs/blob/main/The_Great_Hall_Rules.pdf){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

By joining the Discord or any of our servers, you agree to follow the rules. Most of it is common sense: treat people the way you'd want to be treated.

## Everywhere (Rulebook Section 2)

These apply on every server, in every Discord channel, and in every voice chat.

**Respect & conduct**

- Be civil. No personal attacks, harassment, or bullying.
- No hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory "jokes."
- Keep it appropriate. This is an all-ages community, so no explicit or NSFW content.
- No doxxing. Sharing someone's personal information is an instant permanent ban.

**Fair play**

- No cheating, hacking, modified clients, X-ray, or exploit abuse. Found an exploit? Report it.
- No real-money trading for in-game items, currency, or services.
- Don't deliberately ruin someone else's game.

**Communication**

- English in public channels and in-game chat.
- No spam, no advertising without staff permission, no impersonation.

**Your account**

- You're responsible for anything done on your account. Don't share your login.
- One account per player unless staff says otherwise.
- Keep usernames, skins, and avatars appropriate.

**In-game etiquette**

- Don't enter someone's base without permission, even if it's open.
- Taking something without asking is stealing, even if you meant to give it back.
- Don't steal from shops.
- Leave a reasonable buffer between your build and someone else's.
- Don't wall off or monopolize resources, community areas, or game objectives.

## Discord (Rulebook Section 3)

- Use the right channel. Each game world has its own category.
- No @everyone or @here pings unless you're staff.
- Keep bot commands in the bot channel.
- No mini-modding. Report problems to staff instead.
- No malicious links (phishing, IP loggers, malware). This is an instant permanent ban.
- In voice: no mic spam or disruptive soundboards, and don't record without consent.
- Your game access is linked to your Discord account. If you leave or are banned from the Discord, your game access goes too.
