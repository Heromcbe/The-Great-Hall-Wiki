## Staff Roles

| Role | What they do |
|------|--------------|
| **Head Admin** | Full authority over the community. Makes final decisions. |
| **Server Admin** | Handles day-to-day running of the servers. Escalates permanent bans to a Head Admin. |

## How Rule Breaks Are Handled

Enforcement usually follows these steps:

1. **Verbal warning**
2. **Formal warning**
3. **Temporary ban** (3 to 14 days)
4. **Permanent ban**

Staff may skip steps depending on how serious the rule break is.

## Instant Permanent Bans

Some actions lead straight to a permanent ban with **no appeal**, including:

- Doxxing (sharing someone's personal information)
- Threats against any member
- Sexual content involving minors
- DDoS attacks or other attacks on our servers
- Compromising or attempting to compromise a server
