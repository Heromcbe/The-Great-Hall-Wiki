# FAQ

??? question "How do I join a server?"
    Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/PNdRF6eg5j) and complete onboarding. Your onboarding ticket has everything you need. See [Join the Discord](join-discord.md).

??? question "Where can I find the server address?"
    In your Discord onboarding ticket. Addresses are never posted publicly.

??? question "Why is everything whitelisted?"
    It keeps our servers safe and our community small and friendly. Whitelisting happens through Discord onboarding.

??? question "Which Minecraft servers can I play on Bedrock (console/mobile)?"
    Both. Honeydew Homes is Bedrock-only, and Aethelgard supports both Java and Bedrock. Console players join through **The Great Hall Hub**. See the [Hub guide](../hub/index.md).

??? question "Where are the rules?"
    The [full rulebook (PDF)](https://github.com/Heromcbe/The-Great-Hall-Docs/blob/main/The_Great_Hall_Rules.pdf) is the official version. The [Community Rules](community-rules.md) page is a short summary.

??? question "I lost my roles. What happened?"
    Roles are removed after 60 days of inactivity. See the [Inactivity Policy](inactivity.md), then ask in the Discord to get them back.
