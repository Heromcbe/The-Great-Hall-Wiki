# Weapons & Armor

Better on Bedrock adds several new gear tiers above and alongside vanilla. On Honeydew, HeroBoB+ fills in the gaps and makes **Enderite** the top tier.

## Gear Tiers

| Tier | Tools & weapons | Armor | Full-set bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amethyst** | Sword, pickaxe, axe, spear | Yes | Knocks back melee attackers |
| **Shulker** | None | Yes | Endermen and Shulkers won't attack |
| **Corstinite** | All 5 tools + spear ([HeroBoB+](../herobob-plus/tools-and-weapons/corstinite.md)) | Yes | Fire Resistance |
| **Stardust** | All 5 tools + spear | Yes | Health Boost III, Resistance III |
| **Enderite** | All 5 tools + spear ([HeroBoB+](../herobob-plus/tools-and-weapons/enderite.md)) | Yes | Health Boost IV, Resistance III, Speed II, Jump Boost II |

Full armor stats are on [Armor & Set Bonuses](../herobob-plus/armor-and-set-bonuses.md).

## Crafting Each Tier

| Tier | How it's made |
|---|---|
| Amethyst | Amethyst Shards, same shapes as vanilla gear |
| Shulker | Shulker Shells plus a Persisting Embodiment |
| Corstinite | Corstinite Ingots, same shapes as vanilla gear |
| Stardust | Smithing table upgrade from diamond tools or netherite armor. See [Upgrade Path](upgrade-path.md) |
| Enderite | Enderite Ingots, or upgrade Stardust gear at the Forge Table. See [Upgrade Path](upgrade-path.md) |

## Other Weapons

- **Spears:** throwable spears in 10 materials. See [Spears](../herobob-plus/spears.md).
- **Firey weapons:** pre-enchanted sword, axe, and spear from the Forge Table. See [Firey](../herobob-plus/tools-and-weapons/firey.md).
- **Bane Spike:** poisons what it hits, and the poison stacks. See [Bane Spike](../herobob-plus/enchantments/bane-spike.md).
