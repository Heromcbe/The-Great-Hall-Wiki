# Ores & Materials

Better on Bedrock adds five ores. They feed the new gear tiers and the Forge Table.

## Ores

| Ore | Found in | Pickaxe needed | Drops | Turns into |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tin** | Overworld stone | Stone or better | Raw Tin | **Tin Ingot** (smelt) |
| **Aluminum** | Overworld stone | Stone or better | Raw Aluminum | **Aluminum Ingot** (smelt) |
| **Stardust** | Overworld stone and deepslate | Diamond or better | Stardust Nuggets | **Stardust** (smelt) |
| **Corstinite** | The Nether (netherrack) | Diamond or better | **Corstinite Ingots**, ready to use | |
| **Enderium** | The End (end stone) | Netherite or Stardust | Enderium | **Enderium Smelted** (smelt) |

Fortune increases the drops. Tin, Aluminum, Corstinite, and Stardust can also be packed into storage blocks (9 ingots each).

## Crafted Materials

### Stardust Ingot

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Diamond | Gold Ingot | Diamond |
| Stardust | Gold Ingot | Stardust |
| Stardust | Stardust | Stardust |

Makes **4 Stardust Ingots**.

### Enderite Ingot

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| End Shard | Enderium Smelted | Stardust Ingot |
| Enderium Smelted | End Scroll | Enderium Smelted |
| Stardust Ingot | Enderium Smelted | End Shard |

Makes **1 Enderite Ingot**. End Shards and End Scrolls come from End structures and Endermen. The **Ender Merchant** also trades Enderite Ingots. See [Merchants](merchants.md).

### Firey Ingot

Made at a crafting table from iron, tin, aluminum, and a lava bucket, or from Corstinite. See [Hot Metal](../herobob-plus/forge-table/hot-metal.md).

## What Each Material Is For

| Material | Used for |
|---|---|
| Tin Ingot | Forged pickaxe alloys (Eutectic, Bronze, Tin Plate), Firey Ingots, Miner's Bench |
| Aluminum Ingot | Forged pickaxe alloys (Purple Gold, Ferroaluminum), Firey Ingots, Forge Table |
| Corstinite Ingot | [Corstinite](../herobob-plus/tools-and-weapons/corstinite.md) tools, armor, and spear; Firey Ingots |
| Stardust Ingot | Stardust Spear, template copies, Enderite Ingots |
| Enderite Ingot | [Enderite](../herobob-plus/tools-and-weapons/enderite.md) gear and the Enderite Upgrade |

!!! warning "Three Ender metals"
    **Enderium** (raw) → **Enderium Smelted** (furnace) → **Enderite Ingot** (crafted). Only the **Enderite Ingot** is used for Enderite gear.
