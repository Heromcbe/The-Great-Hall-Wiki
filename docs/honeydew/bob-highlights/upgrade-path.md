# Upgrade Path

How to go from diamond gear to the best gear on Honeydew.

## The Ladder

```
Diamond  ──►  Netherite (armor)  ──►  Stardust  ──►  Enderite
```

## Step 1: Stardust

Stardust gear is made at a **smithing table**, like netherite.

| Slot | Item |
|---|---|
| Template | **Stardust Upgrade Template** |
| Base | **Diamond** tool, or **Netherite** armor piece |
| Material | **Netherite Ingot** |

- Tools (sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe) start from **diamond**.
- Armor starts from **netherite**.
- The **Stardust Spear** is crafted directly: Stardust Ingot, Flint, and Stick in one column.

**Stardust Upgrade Templates** are found in **bastion chests**. You can copy one at a crafting table:

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Deepslate | Stardust Upgrade Template | Deepslate |
| Deepslate | Stardust Ingot | Deepslate |
| Deepslate | Deepslate | Deepslate |

Makes **2 templates**.

## Step 2: Enderite

Enderite is the top tier. There are two ways to get it:

- **Upgrade your Stardust gear** at the Forge Table. You keep all your enchantments. See [Enderite Upgrade](../herobob-plus/forge-table/enderite-upgrade.md).
- **Craft it new** from Enderite Ingots. See [Enderite](../herobob-plus/tools-and-weapons/enderite.md).

!!! tip "Keep your enchantments"
    Enchant your Stardust gear first, then upgrade it to Enderite at the Forge Table. Every enchantment carries over.
