# Better on Bedrock Highlights

**Better on Bedrock** (BoB) is the big addon that powers Honeydew: new biomes, mobs, ores, gear, and more. Its own guide is hard to use, so this section covers the things that matter most on Honeydew.

!!! note "Honeydew runs a tuned version"
    Our own addon, [HeroBoB+](../herobob-plus/index.md), builds on top of Better on Bedrock and improves it. This guide describes Better on Bedrock **as it works on Honeydew**.
