# Bosses

Better on Bedrock adds several bosses. On Honeydew, you fight them at the **Hero Boss Arena**: pay Pence to summon the boss you want, beat it, and get your Pence back.

| Boss | Summon cost (Pence) |
|---|---|
| Piglin Brute | 500 |
| The Willager | 1,000 |
| Enchantaegis | 1,200 |
| Flender | 2,000 |
| Withered Samurai | 2,500 |
| Shielded Pyroclast | 2,500 |
| The Seeker | 3,000 |

Each boss drops its usual Better on Bedrock boss items.
