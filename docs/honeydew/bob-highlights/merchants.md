# Merchants

Better on Bedrock adds wandering traders with rare goods. On Honeydew, HeroBoB+ improves them.

## Goblin Trader

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Anywhere in the Overworld. Spawns like any Better on Bedrock mob |
| **Restocks** | Twice a day (morning and afternoon), like villagers |
| **Keep one** | Put a **name tag** on it and it won't despawn |

Each Goblin Trader offers a random selection of trades. Highlights:

- **Enchantment books** for the Miner's Bench: Vein Miner, Tree Capitator, Leafy Liberator, Harvest Touch, and Auto Smelter (6 Gold Ingots each)
- **One HeroBoB+ book:** Auto Replant, Vein Excavator, or Boring (6 Gold Ingots)
- **Netherite:** Ancient Debris for Netherite Scrap, or Diamonds and Gold Blocks for Netherite Ingots
- **Golden Apples**, Golden Carrots, Name Tags, maps, and more
- **Rare plushies:** Enchanter, Lich, and Willager plushies (16 Diamonds, one per trader)

## Ender Merchant

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The End, on void grass |

Highlights:

- **Enderite Upgrade Templates**
- **Enderite Ingots:** 2 Ancient Debris for 2 Enderite Ingots
- **Netherite Ingots** and **Bane Spikes**
- **Exotic saplings:** Chorus, Voiding, Fungal, Vacant, and Abyss (5 Emeralds each, two offered at a time)

## Librarian Villagers

On Honeydew, Librarians also sell Better on Bedrock and HeroBoB+ enchantment books, including **Auto Replant** and **Vein Excavator**.
