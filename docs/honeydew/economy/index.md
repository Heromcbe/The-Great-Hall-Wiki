# Pence & the Guild Hall

**Pence** is Honeydew's currency. You earn it by selling what you gather, and spend it on boss fights, effect boosts, and the casino.

!!! note "Pence and coins"
    Some in-game menus call your money "coins." It's the same thing: your Pence balance.

## Earning Pence

| Where | What it buys | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **The Bank** | Ores and gems | Inside the Guild Hall |
| **Mass Sell Station** | Crops, logs, and ores in bulk | The gold block near the dock |

Mining pays at the Bank. Farms and tree farms pay at the Mass Sell Station, which is built for selling in bulk.
