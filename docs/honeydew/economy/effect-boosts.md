# Effect Boosts

The Effect Boost NPCs in the **Guild Hall** (X -112, Z -85) sell long-lasting potion effects for Pence. Prices are shown at the NPCs.

## Available Boosts

| Boost | Levels |
|---|---|
| Speed | I, II, III, IV, V |
| Haste | I, II, III, IV, V |
| Jump Boost | I, II, III, IV, V |
| Health Boost | I, II, III, IV, V |

### Specials

| Boost | Level |
|---|---|
| Water Breathing | I |
| Fire Resistance | I |
| Night Vision | I |
| Slow Falling | I |

## Good to Know

- Each level is its own purchase.

!!! warning "Boosts end when you die"
    Dying removes all of your purchased boosts.
