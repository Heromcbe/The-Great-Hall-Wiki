# Casino

The Casino is inside the **Guild Hall** (X -112, Z -85). Walk up to a dealer, pick your bet, and play. All bets and winnings are in Pence.

!!! note "Coins = Pence"
    The casino menus say "coins." That's your Pence balance.

## Games

| Game | Bets |
|---|---|
| Slot Machine | 100 / 150 / 200 |
| Blackjack | 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 |
| Roulette | 2,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 |
| High Roller Blackjack | 2,000 / 4,000 / 6,000 |
| High Stakes Roulette | 5,000 / 7,500 / 10,000 |

## Slot Machine

Spin three reels. Match symbols to win.

| Result | You get back |
|---|---|
| Any pair | 1.1× your bet |
| Three 🍒 | 3× your bet |
| Three 💎 | 4× your bet |
| Three ⭐ | 5× your bet |
| Three 7 | 7× your bet |
| Three 🔔 | 10× your bet |

## Blackjack

Standard Blackjack against the dealer. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

- **Hit** to take a card, **Stand** to hold.
- **Double Down** on your first two cards: double your bet, take one card, and stand.

| Result | You get back |
|---|---|
| Win | 2× your bet |
| Blackjack | 2.5× your bet |
| Push (tie) | Your bet |

### High Roller Blackjack

Same game, bigger bets, and a bigger Blackjack payout: **3.5× your bet**.

## Roulette

Pick a colour, then pick odd or even. The wheel lands on 0 to 36.

- 🔴 **Red** = 1 to 18
- ⚫ **Black** = 19 to 36
- 🟢 **Green** = 0

| Result | You get back |
|---|---|
| Colour **and** odd/even right | Your bet + 1.8× your bet |
| Only one right | Your bet |
| Both wrong | Nothing |
| Bet on Green and it hits | Your bet + 35× your bet |

!!! warning "Stay at the table"
    Walking more than 10 blocks away while the wheel is spinning forfeits your bet.

### High Stakes Roulette

Same rules as Roulette, with bets of 5,000 to 10,000.
