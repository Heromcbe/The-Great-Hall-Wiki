# Honeydew FAQ

??? question "Can I play on Java Edition?"
    No. Honeydew Homes is **Bedrock only** (console, mobile, and Windows). Java players can try [Aethelgard](../aethelgard/index.md) when it launches.

??? question "How do I earn Pence?"
    Sell ores and gems at the **Bank** in the Guild Hall (X -112, Z -85), or sell crops, logs, and ores in bulk at the **Mass Sell Station** (the gold block near the dock). See [Pence & the Guild Hall](economy/index.md).

??? question "Do effect boosts last forever?"
    They last until you die. Dying removes all of your purchased boosts. See [Effect Boosts](economy/effect-boosts.md).

??? question "Where can I find Better on Bedrock structures?"
    They're spread across the world. Use the [World Map](world-map.md) to scout. See [Structures](bob-highlights/structures.md).

??? question "How do I fight a boss?"
    Go to the Hero Boss Arena (X -210, Z 289) and talk to the Arena Master. Summoning costs Pence, and you get it back if you win. See [Hero Bosses](hero-bosses/index.md).

??? question "What's the best gear?"
    **Enderite.** Upgrade your Stardust gear at the Forge Table to keep its enchantments. See the [Upgrade Path](bob-highlights/upgrade-path.md).

??? question "Why doesn't Tree Capitator (or Vein Miner) work?"
    You're probably sneaking. These enchantments switch off while you crouch. Also check that it was applied at the **Miner's Bench**: it shows as a line in the tool's description.

??? question "My sapling won't grow. Why?"
    Custom saplings need room, and some need light level 8 or higher. They also won't grow into anything player-built. Try bone meal, or clear more space around it. See [Trees & Saplings](herobob-plus/trees-and-saplings/index.md).

??? question "Why did the server just restart?"
    Honeydew restarts once a day early in the morning (Eastern time). You get a two-minute warning in chat first. The [World Map](world-map.md) updates right after.

??? question "Something's broken or I found a bug. Who do I tell?"
    Report it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/PNdRF6eg5j).
