# Getting Started

New to Honeydew Homes? Here's what to do on your first day.

## 1. Read the Rules

Honeydew follows the [Community Rules](../network/community-rules.md) plus its own survival rules. The short version is on the [Honeydew page](index.md#rules).

## 2. Find a Place to Build

Honeydew is huge, about 17,000 × 17,000 blocks. Use the [World Map](world-map.md) to scout a spot, and leave a buffer next to other players' builds.

## 3. Visit the Guild Hall

The **Guild Hall** at **X -112, Z -85** is the heart of Honeydew's economy:

- **The Bank:** sell ores and gems for **Pence**
- **Effect Boost NPCs:** buy Speed, Haste, and more
- **The Casino:** try your luck

The **Mass Sell Station** (the gold block near the dock, around **X -60, Z 10**) buys crops, logs, and ores in bulk. See [Pence & the Guild Hall](economy/index.md).

## 4. Know What's New

Honeydew isn't vanilla. A few things worth knowing early:

- **New ores:** tin, aluminum, stardust, corstinite, and enderium. See [Ores & Materials](bob-highlights/ores-and-materials.md).
- **Backpacks:** craft one early for extra storage. See [Backpacks](bob-highlights/backpacks.md).
- **Forge Table:** make tougher pickaxes from your first iron and copper. See [Forge Table](herobob-plus/forge-table/index.md).
- **Miner's Bench:** special enchantments like Tree Capitator and Vein Miner. See [Enchantments](herobob-plus/enchantments/index.md).
- **Durability on your tools:** your tools show how much life they have left. See [Durability Display](herobob-plus/durability-display.md).

## 5. Plan Your Gear

Diamond is just the start. See the [Upgrade Path](bob-highlights/upgrade-path.md) for the climb to **Enderite**, Honeydew's top gear.

## 6. Test Yourself

When you're geared up, summon a boss at the [Hero Boss Arena](hero-bosses/index.md). Beat it and you get your Pence back.

!!! tip "Daily restart"
    The server restarts once a day early in the morning (Eastern time). You'll get a warning in chat two minutes before.
