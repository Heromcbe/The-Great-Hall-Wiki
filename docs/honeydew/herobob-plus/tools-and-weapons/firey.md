# Firey

Firey weapons are forged from lava-infused **Firey Ingots** and come out of the forge already enchanted.

| Weapon | Damage | Durability | Built-in enchantments |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firey Sword | 7 | 1,562 | Fire Aspect II, Looting III |
| Firey Axe | 9 | 1,562 | Efficiency V, Fortune III |
| Firey Spear | 8 | 1,562 | Fire Aspect II, Looting III |

- **Made at the Forge Table** only. See [Hot Metal](../forge-table/hot-metal.md) for the recipes.
- The enchantments are real enchantments. You can add more on top of them.
- **Doesn't burn** in lava or fire.
- **Repair** with a Firey Ingot at an anvil.
- The Firey Spear can be thrown and returns to you. See [Spears](../spears.md).
