# Enderite

Enderite is the **top gear tier on Honeydew**. HeroBoB+ makes every Enderite item stronger than its Stardust version, adds an Enderite shovel, hoe, spear, and full armor set, and gives the armor a powerful set bonus.

Wear all four pieces for **Health Boost IV, Resistance III, Speed II, and Jump Boost II**. See [Armor & Set Bonuses](../armor-and-set-bonuses.md).

## How to Get Enderite Gear

**Option 1: Upgrade your Stardust gear (keeps enchantments).** Use the Forge Table's [Enderite Upgrade](../forge-table/enderite-upgrade.md). Every Stardust tool, spear, and armor piece can be upgraded.

**Option 2: Craft it** at a normal crafting table with **Enderite Ingots**, in the same shape as the diamond version:

| Item | Enderite Ingots | Sticks |
|---|---|---|
| Sword | 2 | 1 |
| Pickaxe | 3 | 2 |
| Axe | 3 | 2 |
| Shovel | 1 | 2 |
| Hoe | 2 | 2 |
| Helmet | 5 | — |
| Chestplate | 8 | — |
| Leggings | 7 | — |
| Boots | 4 | — |

## Good to Know

- **Mines everything**, including obsidian and ancient debris.
- **Doesn't burn** in lava or fire.
- **Repair** with an **Enderite Ingot** at an anvil.

!!! warning "Enderite Ingot only"
    Better on Bedrock has three Ender-metal items: Enderium, Enderium Smelted, and **Enderite Ingot**. Crafting and repair use the **Enderite Ingot**.
