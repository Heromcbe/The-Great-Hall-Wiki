# Trees & Saplings

Better on Bedrock fills Honeydew with unusual trees, but you couldn't grow them yourself. HeroBoB+ adds saplings for them, so you can plant the trees you find in the world.

## Growing Tips

- Saplings grow over time on their own, like vanilla saplings.
- **Bone meal** makes a custom sapling grow right away, as long as it has room.
- **Palm, Orange, Peach, and Poggy** saplings need **light level 8 or higher**. The End and Nether-style trees (Chorus, Voiding, Vacant, Fungal, Abyss) grow in the dark.
- Big trees need room. Leave plenty of open space above and around the sapling.

## Growth Protection

Custom saplings **won't grow into your builds**. Before a tree grows, the space it needs is checked. If anything player-placed is in the way, the sapling picks a smaller version of the tree that fits, or waits and doesn't grow.
