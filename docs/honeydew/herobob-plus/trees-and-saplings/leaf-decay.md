# Leaf Decay

In Better on Bedrock, the canopies of custom trees used to float in the air after you chopped the trunk. HeroBoB+ makes them decay on their own, like vanilla leaves.

## How It Works

- Chop the trunk of a **Chorus, Voiding, Vacant, Fungal, Abyss, or Palm** tree.
- The canopy blocks that are no longer attached to a log **disappear one by one over 5 to 60 seconds**, so the tree falls apart naturally.
- Decay spreads through the whole canopy, even on tall trees.
- Each decaying block drops its normal items, and has about a **1 in 40 chance** to drop the tree's sapling.

## Orange and Peach Trees

Orange and peach leaves now decay properly and **drop saplings**:

- **Orange leaves:** a small chance for an Orange sapling
- **Peach leaves:** about a **1 in 3** chance for **2 Peach saplings** (peach trees only have a few leaves)

!!! tip "Faster clearing"
    Use [Tree Capitator](../enchantments/tree-capitator.md) on the trunk, then let the canopy decay while you move on to the next tree.
