# Custom Saplings

HeroBoB+ adds saplings for Better on Bedrock's special trees. Each one grows into one of several versions of that tree, picked at random.

## The Saplings

| Sapling | Grows into | Tree versions | Needs light? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Palm | Palm tree with coconuts | 2 | Yes (8+) |
| Orange | Orange tree | 1 | Yes (8+) |
| Peach | Peach tree | 1 | Yes (8+) |
| Chorus | Chorus tree | 4 | No |
| Voiding | Voiding tree | 8 | No |
| Vacant | Vacant tree (small or giant) | 4 | No |
| Fungal | Giant mushroom tree | 8 | No |
| Abyss | Abyss tree | 9 | No |

## Where to Get Them

| Sapling | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Orange** | Break or let orange leaves decay |
| **Peach** | Break or let peach leaves decay. Peach trees are small, so their saplings drop often (about 1 in 3) |
| **Chorus, Voiding, Fungal, Abyss** | Chop the tree's trunk. As the canopy decays, each block has a small chance to drop a sapling |
| **Vacant** | Chop a vacant tree. Its spore bodies can drop the sapling as they decay |
| **Palm** | Chop the jungle-log trunk of a palm tree. Its leaves can drop a palm sapling as they decay |

**Buy them:** the **Ender Merchant** sells Chorus, Voiding, Fungal, Vacant, and Abyss saplings for 5 Emeralds each. It offers two of them at a time.

**Plant a coconut:** use a Coconut on sand to plant a Palm sapling. See [Coconuts & Palms](coconuts-and-palms.md).

See [Leaf Decay](leaf-decay.md) for how canopy decay works.

!!! tip "Vacant trees come in two sizes"
    A vacant sapling grows a giant tree if there's room, or a small one if space is tight.
