# Poggy Trees

**Poggy trees** are big, detailed versions of vanilla trees, built from Better on Bedrock's tree designs.

## How to Grow One

Plant a normal sapling. Each time you place one of the saplings below, there's a **50% chance** it turns into a **Poggy sapling**, which has its own look.

| Plant a... | Poggy sapling grows into | Tree versions |
|---|---|---|
| Oak sapling | Giant oak (forest and plains styles) | 24 |
| Acacia sapling | Giant acacia | 3 |
| Jungle sapling | Giant jungle tree | 6 |
| Pale Oak sapling | Giant pale oak | 8 |
| Birch sapling | Giant birch | 17 |
| Dark Oak sapling | Giant dark oak | 11 |

- Poggy trees need **light level 8 or higher** and lots of space.
- **Swamp bonus:** a Poggy oak planted in a swamp grows a **swamp tree** instead.
- The other 50% of the time, you get a normal vanilla sapling that grows a normal tree.
