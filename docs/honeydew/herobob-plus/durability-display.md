# Durability Display

Tools and weapons show how much durability they have left, right in the item's description:

> Durability: 1450 / 1562

## How It Works

- The line appears after you first **mine a block** or **hit something** with the item.
- It updates every time you mine or hit.
- It's always the last line of the item's description, below any enchantments.
- Works on **all tools and weapons**, including Better on Bedrock and HeroBoB+ items.

!!! note "Spears"
    Throwing a spear doesn't update the line. It updates the next time you mine or hit something in melee.
