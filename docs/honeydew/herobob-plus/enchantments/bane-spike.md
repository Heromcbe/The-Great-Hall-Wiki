# Bane Spike

The **Bane Spike** is a Better on Bedrock weapon that poisons what it hits. HeroBoB+ reworked it so the poison **stacks**.

## How It Works

Each hit makes the poison stronger and last longer:

| Hit | Poison | Time added |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Poison I | 5 seconds |
| 2nd | Poison II | +5 seconds |
| 3rd | Poison III | +5 seconds |
| 4th | Poison IV | +5 seconds |
| 5th and beyond | Poison V (max) | +5 seconds |

- Poison lasts **20 seconds at most**.
- Once the poison wears off, the next hit starts back at Poison I.
- Keep hitting to keep the stack going.

!!! note "Some mobs are immune"
    Undead mobs, spiders, and some bosses can't be poisoned, so the Bane Spike only does its normal damage to them.
