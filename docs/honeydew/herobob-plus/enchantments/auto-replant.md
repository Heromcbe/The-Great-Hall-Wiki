# Auto Replant

**Auto Replant I** is a hoe enchantment: harvest a fully grown crop and it's replanted for you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tool** | Hoe |
| **Applied at** | Miner's Bench (6 levels + Auto Replant book) |
| **Can't combine with** | Harvest Touch, Silk Touch |
| **Turn it off** | Sneak while harvesting |

## How It Works

1. Break a **fully grown** crop with your hoe.
2. You get the normal harvest.
3. One seed is taken from your inventory and the crop is replanted.

If you don't have the seed in your inventory, the crop isn't replanted. Young crops aren't affected.
