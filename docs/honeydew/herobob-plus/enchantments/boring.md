# Boring

**Boring I** turns your pickaxe into a drill. Every block you mine breaks **27 blocks** in a shape that depends on how you're standing.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tool** | Pickaxe |
| **Applied at** | Miner's Bench (6 levels + Boring book) |
| **Can't combine with** | Vein Miner, Auto Smelter |

## Two Shapes

| Stance | Shape | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Standing** | A tunnel **1 wide, 3 tall, 9 deep** in the direction you're facing | Fast tunnels and branch mining |
| **Sneaking** | A **3×3×3 cube** centred on the block you hit | Clearing out rooms |

The blocks break in a quick cascade, so you can watch the drill work.

## Good to Know

- Only breaks blocks a **pickaxe** is meant for (stone, ores, deepslate, and so on). Dirt, sand, wood, water, and lava are left alone.
- **Fortune** and **Silk Touch** apply to every block.
- Uses **1 durability per block** (Unbreaking helps). If your pickaxe breaks mid-cascade, the rest stops.
- There's **no off switch**. Sneaking changes the shape instead, so keep a normal pickaxe for careful work.

## Getting the Book

Buy it from the **Goblin Trader** for 6 Gold Ingots, when it's in stock.
