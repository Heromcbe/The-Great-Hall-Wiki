# Leafy Liberator

**Leafy Liberator I** clears a whole canopy at once: break one leaf block and every connected leaf breaks with it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tools** | Shears, axes, and hoes |
| **Applied at** | Miner's Bench (6 levels + Leafy Liberator book) |
| **Turn it off** | Sneak while breaking |

## How It Works

- Break a leaf block and all connected leaves of the same type break too, up to **128 blocks**.
- Your tool loses **1 durability per block**, and stops before it would break.

## What Drops

| Tool | You get |
|---|---|
| **Shears** | The leaf blocks themselves |
| **Axe or hoe** | Normal leaf loot: saplings, sticks, and apples (oak and dark oak) |
| **Axe or hoe with Silk Touch** | The leaf blocks themselves |

**Fortune** on an axe or hoe raises the sapling chance.

## What It Works On

- **Every vanilla leaf:** oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, cherry, mangrove, azalea, flowering azalea, **pale oak**, and **poplar** (red, orange, and yellow)
- **Better on Bedrock leaves:** orange and peach leaves, shrub leaves
- **Custom tree canopies:** chorus blocks and plants, slimeshrooms, white mushroom growth, and voided bushes
