# Tree Capitator

**Tree Capitator I** is an axe enchantment: chop one log and the whole connected trunk comes down.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tool** | Axe |
| **Applied at** | Miner's Bench (6 levels + Tree Capitator book) |
| **Turn it off** | Sneak while chopping |

## How It Works

- Break any log and every connected log of the same tree breaks with it, up to **128 blocks**.
- Your axe loses **1 durability per log**. It stops before your axe would break.
- Your axe's Fortune and Silk Touch still apply.

## What It Works On

- **Every vanilla tree:** oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, and **poplar**
- **Nether stems:** crimson and warped
- **Stripped logs and wood blocks** of all of the above
- **Better on Bedrock trees:** chorus, voiding, vacant, fungal, and abyss

## What HeroBoB+ Changed

- Added **poplar**, **pale oak**, and the **fungal** and **abyss** trees, which didn't work before.
- Chopping a custom Better on Bedrock tree now makes its canopy decay on its own. See [Leaf Decay](../trees-and-saplings/leaf-decay.md).
