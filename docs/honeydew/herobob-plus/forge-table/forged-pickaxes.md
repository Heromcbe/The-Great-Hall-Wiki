# Forged Pickaxes

Forged pickaxes are built in three steps at the [Forge Table](index.md). They last longer than crafting-table pickaxes of the same tier, some mine much faster, and the handle takes on the colour of the wood you use.

## How to Forge a Pickaxe

1. **Forge Pickaxe Head:** pick a material. The ingots are taken from your inventory and you get a pickaxe head.
2. **Craft Handle:** pick a wood type. One log becomes a handle in that wood's colour.
3. **Assemble Pickaxe:** choose a head and a handle. They're combined into your finished pickaxe.

You get XP at every step.

## Heads

| Head | Ingredients | Durability | Speed | Mines up to | XP (total) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eutectic | 2 Gold Ingot + 1 Tin Ingot | 95 | 12.0 | Wood tier | 14 |
| Purple Gold | 2 Gold Ingot + 1 Aluminum Ingot | 156 | 14.4 | Stone tier | 16 |
| Forged Copper | 3 Copper Ingot | 206 | 5.0 | Stone tier | 11 |
| Bronze | 2 Copper Ingot + 1 Tin Ingot | 231 | 5.5 | Stone tier | 13 |
| Ferroaluminum | 2 Iron Ingot + 1 Aluminum Ingot | 643 | 6.5 | Iron tier | 19 |
| Forged Iron | 3 Iron Ingot | 906 | 7.0 | Iron tier | 19 |
| Tin Plate | 2 Iron Ingot + 1 Tin Ingot | 1,037 | 7.5 | Iron tier | 19 |

**For comparison:** a normal Iron Pickaxe has 250 durability and speed 6.0. A Diamond Pickaxe has 1,561 durability and speed 8.0.

- **Mines up to** means the hardest blocks it can harvest. Iron tier handles diamond, gold, redstone, and emerald ore, like a normal iron pickaxe.
- **XP (total)** adds up all three steps: head, plus 2 XP for the handle and 2 XP for assembling.

!!! tip "Speed or durability?"
    Eutectic and Purple Gold are **speed** picks: very fast but they wear out quickly. The iron alloys are **workhorse** picks: Tin Plate lasts about four times as long as a normal iron pickaxe.

## Enchanting and Repair

- Forged pickaxes take enchantments like any other pickaxe. Gold-based picks (Eutectic, Purple Gold) enchant best.
- **Repair** at an anvil with the head's main ingot:

| Pickaxe | Repair with |
|---|---|
| Eutectic, Purple Gold | Gold Ingot |
| Forged Copper, Bronze | Copper Ingot |
| Ferroaluminum, Forged Iron, Tin Plate | Iron Ingot |

Each ingot restores a quarter of the pickaxe's durability. You can also combine two of the same pickaxe.

!!! note "Tin and aluminum"
    Tin and aluminum are Better on Bedrock ores. See [Ores & Materials](../../bob-highlights/ores-and-materials.md).
