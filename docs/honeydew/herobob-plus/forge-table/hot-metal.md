# Hot Metal

**Hot Metal** is the Forge Table menu for Firey weapons. Each one comes out of the forge already enchanted.

## Step 1: Make Firey Ingots

Firey Ingots are made at a normal crafting table. There are two recipes. Either one works.

**Recipe 1 (HeroBoB+):**

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Iron Ingot | Tin Ingot | Iron Ingot |
| Aluminum Ingot | Lava Bucket | Aluminum Ingot |
| Iron Ingot | Tin Ingot | Iron Ingot |

Makes **1 Firey Ingot**.

**Recipe 2 (Better on Bedrock):**

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| | Smeared Pearl | Quetzacaw Feather |
| Smeared Pearl | Corstinite Ingot | Smeared Pearl |
| Blackstone Crumb | Smeared Pearl | |

Makes **1 Firey Ingot**.

## Step 2: Forge the Weapon

Open the [Forge Table](index.md), choose **Hot Metal**, and pick a weapon.

| Weapon | Ingredients | Built-in enchantments | Damage | Durability | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Firey Sword** | 2 Firey Ingot + 1 Stick | Fire Aspect II, Looting III | 7 | 1,562 | 20 |
| **Firey Axe** | 3 Firey Ingot + 2 Stick | Efficiency V, Fortune III | 9 | 1,562 | 25 |
| **Firey Spear** | 2 Firey Ingot + 2 Stick | Fire Aspect II, Looting III | 8 | 1,562 | 20 |

## Good to Know

- Firey weapons **don't burn** in lava or fire.
- **Repair** with a Firey Ingot at an anvil, or combine two of the same weapon.
- The Firey Spear can be thrown and returns to you. See [Spears](../spears.md).
