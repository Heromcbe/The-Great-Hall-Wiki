# Enderite Upgrade

Turn your Stardust gear into **Enderite**, the top gear tier on Honeydew. Your enchantments come with it.

## What You Need

- The **Stardust item** you want to upgrade, **held in your main hand**
- **1 Enderite Upgrade Template**
- **2 Enderite Ingots**

## Getting a Template

- **End City chests:** templates can turn up as loot.
- **Ender Merchant:** sells templates.
- **Copy one:** duplicate a template you already have at a crafting table.

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| End Stone | Enderite Upgrade Template | End Stone |
| End Stone | Enderite Ingot | End Stone |
| End Stone | End Stone | End Stone |

Makes **2 templates**, so you keep the one you put in and get a spare.

## How to Upgrade

1. Hold the Stardust item in your main hand.
2. Open the [Forge Table](index.md) and choose **Enderite Upgrade**.
3. The menu shows what you have. Press **Upgrade**.

You get the Enderite version with **all of its enchantments and lore kept**, plus **30 XP**.

## What Can Be Upgraded

| Stardust | Becomes |
|---|---|
| Sword | Enderite Sword |
| Pickaxe | Enderite Pickaxe |
| Axe | Enderite Axe |
| Shovel | Enderite Shovel |
| Hoe | Enderite Hoe |
| Spear | Enderite Spear |
| Helmet | Enderite Helmet |
| Chestplate | Enderite Chestplate |
| Leggings | Enderite Leggings |
| Boots | Enderite Boots |

See [Enderite](../tools-and-weapons/enderite.md) for stats and [Armor & Set Bonuses](../armor-and-set-bonuses.md) for the full-set bonus.

!!! warning "Use Enderite Ingots"
    Better on Bedrock has three Ender-metal items: Enderium, Enderium Smelted, and **Enderite Ingot**. Only the **Enderite Ingot** works for the upgrade.
