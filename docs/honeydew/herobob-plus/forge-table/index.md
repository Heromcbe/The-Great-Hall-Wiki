# Forge Table

The Forge Table is HeroBoB+'s crafting station for gear you can't make at a normal crafting table. Every step you complete at the Forge Table gives you XP.

## Crafting the Forge Table

Craft it at a normal crafting table:

There are two recipes. Either one works.

**Recipe 1 (HeroBoB+):** one column of **Anvil**, **Blast Furnace**, **Anvil**.

## Using It

Interact with the Forge Table to open its menu. Ingredients are taken straight from your inventory, so you don't need to place anything on the table.

| Menu option | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Forge Pickaxe Head** | Turn ingots and alloys into a pickaxe head | [Forged Pickaxes](forged-pickaxes.md) |
| **Craft Handle** | Turn a log into a handle in that wood's colour | [Forged Pickaxes](forged-pickaxes.md) |
| **Assemble Pickaxe** | Combine a head and a handle into a finished pickaxe | [Forged Pickaxes](forged-pickaxes.md) |
| **Hot Metal** | Forge Firey weapons with built-in enchantments | [Hot Metal](hot-metal.md) |
| **Enderite Upgrade** | Upgrade Stardust gear to Enderite, keeping its enchantments | [Enderite Upgrade](enderite-upgrade.md) |

!!! tip "Missing something?"
    If you don't have the ingredients, the menu tells you exactly what you need. Nothing is used up until you confirm.
