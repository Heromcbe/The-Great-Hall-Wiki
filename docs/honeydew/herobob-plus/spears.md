# Spears

Every spear on Honeydew can be **thrown like a trident**. HeroBoB+ took over Better on Bedrock's spear system, fixed it, made every spear repairable, and added three new ones.

## Throwing

- **Hold use** to charge, then **release** to throw.
- **Loyalty** makes the spear fly back to you after it lands.
- **Riptide** launches you forward when you're in water.
- Thrown spears apply their enchantments on hit, including **Fire Aspect, Knockback, Sharpness, Smite, and Bane of Arthropods**.

## Repairing

- At an anvil, each repair item restores **a quarter** of the spear's durability.
- Or combine **two of the same spear** to merge their durability.

## Getting the New Spears

| Spear | How |
|---|---|
| Corstinite | Crafting table, in one column: **Corstinite Ingot** (top), **Flint** (middle), **Stick** (bottom) |
| Firey | Forge Table → [Hot Metal](forge-table/hot-metal.md). Comes with Fire Aspect II and Looting III |
| Enderite | Crafting table, in one column: **Enderite Ingot** (top), **Flint** (middle), **Stick** (bottom). Or [upgrade](forge-table/enderite-upgrade.md) a Stardust Spear at the Forge Table to keep its enchantments |

The other seven spears use Better on Bedrock's recipes.

!!! note "Durability display"
    The durability line on a spear updates when you hit something in melee, but not after a throw. The spear still loses durability normally.
