# Recipes

Extra crafting-table recipes added by HeroBoB+. Forge Table recipes are on the [Forge Table](forge-table/index.md) pages.

## Recolour Concrete

Change the colour of **concrete** or **concrete powder** with any dye.

- **1 concrete (or concrete powder) + 1 dye → 1 block in the dye's colour**
- Works for all 16 colours, in any direction. Example: orange concrete + gray dye = gray concrete.
- Shapeless: place the two items anywhere in the grid.

## Crack a Coconut

- **1 Coconut → 2 Broken Open Coconuts**
