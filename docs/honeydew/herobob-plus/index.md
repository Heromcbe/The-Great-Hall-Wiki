# HeroBoB+

HeroBoB+ is our own addon, built for Honeydew Homes. It adds new gear and crafting on top of [Better on Bedrock](../bob-highlights/index.md) and fixes a lot of what was broken in it.

## What It Adds

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Forge Table**

    ---

    Forge custom pickaxes, Firey weapons, and upgrade Stardust gear to Enderite.

    [Forge Table](forge-table/index.md)

-   **Tools & Weapons**

    ---

    Corstinite, Enderite, and Firey gear.

    [Corstinite](tools-and-weapons/corstinite.md) · [Enderite](tools-and-weapons/enderite.md) · [Firey](tools-and-weapons/firey.md)

-   **Armor & Set Bonuses**

    ---

    Wear a full set for extra effects.

    [Armor & Set Bonuses](armor-and-set-bonuses.md)

-   **Spears**

    ---

    Throwable spears that come back to you.

    [Spears](spears.md)

-   **Enchantments**

    ---

    New: Boring, Vein Excavator, and Auto Replant. Plus Tree Capitator, Leafy Liberator, and Bane Spike.

    [Enchantments](enchantments/index.md)

-   **Trees & Saplings**

    ---

    Plant the custom trees you find in the world.

    [Trees & Saplings](trees-and-saplings/index.md)

</div>

## Also Included

- [Recipes](recipes.md): extra crafting recipes, like recolouring concrete with dye
- [Durability Display](durability-display.md): see how much life your tools have left
- [Bug Fixes](bug-fixes.md): what we've fixed in Better on Bedrock
