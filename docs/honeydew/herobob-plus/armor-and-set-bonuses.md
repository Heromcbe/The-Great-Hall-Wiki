# Armor & Set Bonuses

Wear all four pieces of a set (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) to get its bonus. Take any piece off and the bonus fades.

## Armor Tiers

From weakest to strongest:

| Set | Helmet | Chestplate | Leggings | Boots | Total protection | Full-set bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amethyst | 2 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 13 | Knocks back enemies that hit you in melee |
| Shulker | 2 | 6 | 3 | 3 | 14 | Endermen and Shulkers won't attack you |
| Corstinite | 3 | 8 | 6 | 3 | 20 | **Fire Resistance** |
| Stardust | 5 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 32 | Health Boost III, Resistance III |
| **Enderite** | 6 | 10 | 10 | 9 | **35** | **Health Boost IV, Resistance III, Speed II, Jump Boost II** |

## What HeroBoB+ Changed

- **Corstinite:** added the Fire Resistance set bonus (it had none).
- **Enderite:** added the full armor set and its set bonus. Enderite is now the top tier. See [Enderite](tools-and-weapons/enderite.md).
- **Amethyst:** fixed the knockback bonus, which didn't work before.

!!! tip "Getting Enderite armor"
    Upgrade Stardust armor at the Forge Table to keep its enchantments. See [Enderite Upgrade](forge-table/enderite-upgrade.md).
