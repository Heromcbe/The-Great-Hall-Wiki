# Hero Bosses

Want a boss fight on your terms? At the **Hero Boss Arena** you pay Pence to summon one of Better on Bedrock's bosses, fight it, and keep the loot.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | X -210, Z 289 |
| **Who to talk to** | The Arena Master, below the arena |
| **Cost** | 500 to 3,000 Pence |
| **Time limit** | 10 minutes |

## How It Works

1. Talk to the **Arena Master** and pick a boss.
2. The cost is taken from your Pence.
3. A 10-second countdown starts, then the boss spawns in the arena.
4. You have **10 minutes** to win.
5. Beat it and **your Pence is refunded**. The boss drops its usual loot.

!!! tip "Win it back"
    Defeat the boss and you get the full summoning cost back. Stay online until the boss dies, or you won't get the refund.

!!! warning "Beat the clock"
    If the boss is still alive after 10 minutes, it disappears and drops nothing. Your Pence is not refunded.

## Tips

- Bring friends. Anyone can help fight, and the refund goes to whoever paid.
- Gear up first. These are real boss fights.
- Need more Pence? See [Pence & the Guild Hall](../economy/index.md).
